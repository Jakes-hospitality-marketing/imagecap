IMAGECAP
Makes image files smaller so they fit upload limits.


There's nothing to install any more. Just open this link:

    https://jakes-hospitality-marketing.github.io/imagecap/

Bookmark it. That's the whole thing.


USING IT
--------

1. Drag your images onto the page. A whole folder works too.

2. Pick a size limit at the top. 1.9 MB is the usual choice.

3. Click Compress.

4. Click "Download all" to get them back as a zip.

Your original images are never changed, moved, or deleted. Nothing is
uploaded anywhere either — the compressing happens on your own computer,
inside the browser.


IPHONE PHOTOS (HEIC)
--------------------

If you drop in photos straight from an iPhone, you may see a message
saying the browser can't read them. That's normal: HEIC is Apple's photo
format and only Safari opens it directly.

You'll get two buttons. Either works:

  - "Open in Safari" — fastest option if Safari is handy.
  - "Convert them here" — stays in the browser you're using. Slower,
    but you don't have to switch.


THE OLD MAC APP
---------------

If you installed the ImageCap app earlier, you can keep using it or drag
it to the Trash — the link above does the same job and is always up to
date. Nothing is lost either way.
