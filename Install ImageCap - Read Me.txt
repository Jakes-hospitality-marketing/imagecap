INSTALLING IMAGECAP
===================

This takes about a minute. You only ever do it once.

Partway through, your Mac will say it can't open the app. That's expected —
it's because ImageCap is an in-house tool and not sold through the App
Store, not because anything is wrong with it. Step 3 is how you get past
that. After this first time, ImageCap opens normally forever, and it
updates itself.


STEP 1 — Copy the app over
--------------------------
Drag ImageCap (the app icon in this folder) into your Applications folder.


STEP 2 — Try to open it
-----------------------
Go to your Applications folder and double-click ImageCap.

Your Mac will show a message saying it can't verify the app, with a
"Done" button. Click Done.

Nothing has gone wrong. Keep going.


STEP 3 — Allow it
-----------------
Do this part within an hour of Step 2, or you'll need to repeat Step 2
first.

  1. Open System Settings (the grey gear icon).
  2. Click "Privacy & Security" in the left-hand sidebar.
  3. Scroll all the way down to the "Security" section.
  4. You'll see a line saying ImageCap "was blocked to protect your Mac."
     Click the "Open Anyway" button next to it.
  5. Enter your Mac password when asked.
  6. If one more window appears, click "Open Anyway" again.

ImageCap will open.


THAT'S IT
---------
You never have to do any of this again. ImageCap now opens with a normal
double-click, and when there's a new version it updates itself.


USING IT
--------
  - Drag images, or a whole folder of images, onto the window.
  - Pick a size limit at the top. 1.9 MB is the usual one.
  - Click "Choose folder" to say where the results should go.
  - Click Compress.

You get a new folder containing the compressed copies. Your original
images are never changed or moved.
