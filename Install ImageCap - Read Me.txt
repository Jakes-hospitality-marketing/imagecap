IMAGECAP
Makes image files smaller so they fit upload limits.


===========================================
INSTALLING — about 2 minutes, one time only
===========================================

Read this first: your Mac WILL refuse to open the app the first time,
and will say it cannot verify the developer. Nothing is wrong. That
message appears for any app not bought from the App Store, and this
one we built ourselves. Step 3 clears it for good.


1. Put ImageCap in your Applications folder.

   Drag the ImageCap icon into Applications.


2. Open your Applications folder and double-click ImageCap.

   A warning appears. Depending on your Mac it says either

       "ImageCap cannot be opened because it is from an
        unidentified developer"

   or

       "Apple could not verify ImageCap is free of malware"

   Click  OK  (or  Done  — whichever button it shows).

   If you see a "Move to Trash" button, do not click it.


3. Now allow it.

   Open System Settings.
   Click  Privacy & Security  in the left sidebar.
   Scroll all the way down to the bottom.

   You'll see a line saying ImageCap was blocked,
   with a button next to it:

       Click  Open Anyway
       Enter your Mac password
       If one more box appears, click  Open Anyway  again

   ImageCap opens.


That's it. From now on it opens with a normal double-click, and it
updates itself when there's a new version.

Timing note: do step 3 within an hour of step 2. If you leave it
longer the button disappears — just double-click ImageCap again
and redo step 3.


==========
USING IT
==========

1. Drag your images onto the window. A whole folder works too.

2. Pick a size limit at the top. 1.9 MB is the usual choice.

3. Click "Choose folder" to pick where the results go.

4. Click Compress.

You get a new folder with the smaller copies inside. Your original
images are never changed, moved, or deleted.
