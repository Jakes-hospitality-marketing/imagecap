IMAGECAP
Makes image files smaller so they fit upload limits.


===========================================
INSTALLING — about 2 minutes, one time only
===========================================

Heads up before you start: partway through, your Mac will say it
can't open the app. That is normal and expected. It happens because
this is a tool we made ourselves rather than something bought from
the App Store. Step 3 clears it permanently.


1. Drag ImageCap into your Applications folder.


2. Open your Applications folder and double-click ImageCap.

   A box appears saying Apple could not verify the app.

   Click  Done
   Do NOT click "Move to Trash".


3. Open System Settings, then click Privacy & Security.

   Scroll down to the very bottom.

   You'll see a line about ImageCap being blocked.

   Click  Open Anyway
   Enter your Mac password.
   If another box appears, click  Open Anyway  again.


ImageCap opens. You're done — it will open normally from now on,
and it updates itself when there's a new version.

One catch: do step 3 within an hour of step 2. If you wait longer,
just double-click ImageCap again and redo step 3.


==========
USING IT
==========

1. Drag your images onto the window. A whole folder works too.

2. Pick a size limit at the top. 1.9 MB is the usual choice.

3. Click "Choose folder" to pick where the results go.

4. Click Compress.

You get a new folder with the smaller copies inside. Your original
images are never changed, moved, or deleted.
